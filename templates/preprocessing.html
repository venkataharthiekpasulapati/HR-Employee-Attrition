Module 2 Engineering

HR Data Preprocessing & Feature Engineering

The systematic transformation pipeline from raw corporate HR records into model-ready numerical feature tensors. Adhering to strict training-serving boundary discipline to eliminate leakage and skew.

1️⃣

Constant Feature Elimination

Identified zero-variance constants (EmployeeCount=1, StandardHours=80, Over18='Y') and unique identifier EmployeeNumber, automatically dropping them from the feature space.

2️⃣

Categorical One-Hot Encoding

Nominal attributes (Department, EducationField, JobRole, MaritalStatus, OverTime) encoded via OneHotEncoder with handle_unknown='ignore'.

3️⃣

Standardization & Scaling

Continuous financial and tenure metrics (MonthlyIncome, DailyRate, TotalWorkingYears) scaled via StandardScaler ($z = \frac{x - \mu}{\sigma}$).

4️⃣

Class Imbalance Mitigation

With 16.1% turnover rate, balanced class weights ($w_j = \frac{N}{2 N_j}$) are applied across Logistic Regression and Random Forest models to penalize false negative attrition.
